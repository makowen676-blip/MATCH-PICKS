Picks come from API-Football’s win chances plus a goals model built from each team’s scoring averages, or from bookmaker odds in ESPN mode. They are estimates, not certainties. Every chance is shown, large or small. Each pick is ranked by the model chance, how often the bet landed in recent games, and bookmaker price value, not by percentage alone. Obvious lines like Over 0.5 goals are listed but not chosen as the headline. This app only shows estimates and never places bets. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
